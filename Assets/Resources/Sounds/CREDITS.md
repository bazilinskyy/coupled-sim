# Sound recordings

All recordings here are from [Freesound](https://freesound.org) and in the public domain:
[Creative Commons 0 (CC0 1.0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (the licence was checked on
each sound's page). No attribution is required; the authors are credited here with thanks.

Changes for the simulator: made from Freesound's high-quality previews (MP3), mixed to mono at 48 kHz, cut, high-pass
filtered (below 18-30 Hz; the bicycle ride below 150 Hz to remove the recorder's wind rumble; the birds below 0.8-1.2 kHz),
made into seamless loops (the end cross-faded into the start) or cut into single steps, and normalised. `SoundClips`
loads them (`Resources.Load`) and `Soundscape` sets their levels from its models; without them it synthesises the sounds.
